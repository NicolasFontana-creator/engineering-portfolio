### Electronic Engineering Student Portfolio

I am an advanced **Electronic Engineering** student at **Universidad Tecnológica Nacional, Facultad Regional Paraná (UTN-FRP)**, Argentina. This portfolio brings together academic projects and technical work related to telecommunications, network infrastructure, wireless systems, and electronics.

Its purpose is to present the technical problem, engineering methodology, tools, and documented outcomes of each project, clearly distinguishing between designed, simulated, implemented, and experimentally evaluated work.

## Technical knowledge

- **Telecommunications:** FTTH/GPON fiber-optic networks, microwave radio links, and mobile networks.
- **Networks and infrastructure:** IP networking, network services, and virtualization.
- **Wireless communications and IoT:** LoRaWAN, MQTT, and telemetry.
- **Engineering tools:** MATLAB, Node-RED, InfluxDB, Grafana, and data analysis.
